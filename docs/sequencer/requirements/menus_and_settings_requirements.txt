HEXBOARD SEQUENCER MENU AND SETTINGS REQUIREMENTS

ID: SEQ-MENU-001
Title: Main Sequencer Menu
Status: Active
Area: Menus
Priority: High

Requirement:
The main Sequencer menu shall include the current top-level Sequencer actions
and settings.

Details:
- Keyboard
- Playback Settings
- Synth Options
- File Management
- New
- Save
- Save New
- Load
- Revert
- Seq Lights
- Color Options
- Tap Preview
- Update Firmware

ID: SEQ-MENU-016
Title: Shared Color Options Access
Status: Active
Area: Menus
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Sequencer mode shall provide access to Color Options from the Sequencer menu.

Details:
- The Sequencer Color Options submenu shall include:
  Color Mode, Brightness, Animation, Rest Bright, and Dim Bright.
- These controls shall use the same underlying board profile settings as the
  Keyboard Color Options menu.
- Changing a Color Options setting from Keyboard mode or Sequencer mode shall
  update the same shared value.
- Animation shall also control the lower playable keyboard LEDs that Sequencer
  transport playback triggers for currently sounding notes.
- Rest Bright shall control normal resting note brightness and regular
  sequencer step-light brightness.
- Dim Bright shall control out-of-scale note brightness only while Scale Lock
  is Off.


ID: SEQ-MENU-017
Title: Seq Lights Submenu
Status: Active
Area: Menus
Priority: High

Requirement:
Sequencer mode shall provide a Seq Lights submenu for sequencer step-light
preferences.

Details:
- The Seq Lights submenu shall include:
  Accent Every, Step Color, and Step Hue.
- Accent Every shall provide:
  Off, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.
- Accent Every shall emphasize every Nth step, starting from step 1.
- Accent Every shall use brightness changes only and shall not change step hue.
- The default Accent Every value shall be 4.
- Step Color shall provide:
  Note and Regular.
- The default Step Color value shall be Regular.
- Sequencer step lights shall use this brightness ladder:
  off, low, medium, high, highest.
- The current step mapping shall be:
  empty step without accent = off
  empty step with accent = medium
  step with note = medium
  accented step with note = high
  selected step with note = high
  playing step with note = highest.
- A tied step shall use the same programmed-step brightness behavior as a step
  with a note.
- During transport playback, the lower playable keyboard LEDs shall light the
  notes that are currently sounding for their full held duration.
- In Step Color = Note, programmed steps shall use note-based colors with that
  brightness ladder.
- In Step Color = Regular, programmed steps shall use the selected Step Hue
  with that brightness ladder.
- Empty unselected steps without accent shall stay off in both modes.
- A selected or playing empty step shall light at the highest level so
  selection and playback remain visible.
- Step Hue shall be available only while Step Color = Regular.
- Step Hue shall provide:
  Red, Orange, Yellow, Lime, Green, Teal, Cyan, Lt Blue, Blue, Indigo,
  Purple, Magenta, Pink.
- The default Step Hue value shall be Indigo.
- Step Hue shall preview live while its menu choices are scrolled before the
  selection is confirmed.
- Seq Lights settings shall be stored in the board profile settings rather than
  in the current sequence file.

ID: SEQ-MENU-011
Title: Shared Menu Navigation Buttons
Status: Active
Area: Menus
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
When a menu page is active and the bottom command button below the encoder is
held, the top two command buttons below the encoder shall act like one encoder
step up and one encoder step down until that bottom button is released.

Details:
- During normal menu navigation, the top shortcut button shall navigate up and
  the lower shortcut button shall navigate down.
- While editing a value, the top shortcut button shall increase the value and
  the lower shortcut button shall decrease the value.
