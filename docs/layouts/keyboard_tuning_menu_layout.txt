KEYBOARD TUNING MENU LAYOUT

Purpose:
- Shows the available keyboard tunings.
- Clearly marks the currently selected tuning.

Current behavior:
- Opening `Keyboard > Tuning` places the menu cursor on the current tuning.
- The current tuning row is prefixed with `* `.
- Non-selected tunings are prefixed with two spaces.
- The rows below the tuning list keep their normal labels and are not marked with `* `.

Example:

```text
Tuning

* 12 EDO
  19 EDO
  24 EDO
  Bohlen-Pierce
  Dynamic JI
  JI BPM Lock
  JI BPM
```

Notes:
- The `* ` marker applies only to actual tuning choices.
- Other settings on the page do not use the current-choice marker.
