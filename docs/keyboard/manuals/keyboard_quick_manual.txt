HEXBOARD KEYBOARD MODE
QUICK MANUAL

Confidence

This is a first-pass Keyboard manual reconstructed from the current firmware
code and comments in `src/HexBoard.ino`.

Overview

Keyboard mode is the board's normal playing mode. The playable hexes follow the
current tuning, layout, scale, key, and transpose settings. The OLED menu lets
you change the board-wide keyboard settings and enter Sequencer mode.

Direct Playing

- Non-command hexes play notes.
- The pitch mapping follows the current tuning, layout, key, and transpose.
- When `Scale Lock` is On, only in-scale note keys respond.
- When `Scale Lock` is Off, all playable note keys respond.
- `Transpose` shifts the sounded pitches without changing the layout geometry.
- Shared setting: `DisplayNotes` can show the notes you are holding on the
  OLED.

Command Buttons And Wheels

- The board has 7 command buttons beside the main playable field.
- The top 3 command buttons act as the velocity wheel.
- The middle command button toggles the lower wheel between modulation and
  pitch bend.
- The bottom 3 command buttons act as the currently selected lower wheel.
- Pitch Bend and Mod Wheel behavior can each be `Springy` or `Sticky`.
- Velocity, Mod Wheel, and Pitch Bend wheel speeds are adjustable in the menu.

Encoder And Menu

- Turn the encoder to move through menu items and edit values.
- Press the encoder to open a page, confirm a choice, or save an edited value.
- Hold the encoder for about 2 seconds to send a panic stop that silences MIDI
  and synth output.
- When a menu page is open, hold the lowest command button below the encoder to
  make the top two command buttons act as menu up/down shortcuts.

Main Menu

- `Sequencer`: enter Sequencer mode.
- `Tuning`: choose the tuning and the just intonation options.
- `Layout`: choose the current layout for the tuning and adjust mirror/rotate.
- `Scales`: choose `Key`, `Scale Lock`, and the current scale.
- `Color Options`: set color mode, brightness, animation, rest brightness, and
  dim brightness. `Dim Bright` affects out-of-scale note LEDs only when
  `Scale Lock` is Off.
- `Synth Options`: set synth mode, waveform, envelope, arpeggiator settings,
  and on hardware v1.2 the audio output destination.
- `MIDI Options`: set MIDI channel, MPE behavior, CC 74 value, and preset
  program-change lists.
- `Control Wheel`: adjust wheel speeds and springy/sticky behavior.
- `Transpose`: shift played pitches from `-127` to `+127` tuning steps.
- `Save`: save the current settings into a profile slot and choose Auto-Save.
- `Load`: load a saved profile slot.
- `Advanced`: firmware info, hardware info, invert encoder, `ColorByKey`,
  `DisplayNotes`, reset, update firmware, delegated mode, and debug.

Tuning, Layout, And Scales

- Tuning choices are firmware-defined and include standard and microtonal
  systems such as `12 EDO (Normal)`, `17 EDO`, `31 EDO`, `Bohlen-Pierce`, and
  the Carlos tunings.
- Choosing a new tuning resets `Layout` to that tuning's first layout, `Scale`
  to `None`, and `Key` to `C`.
- Layout choices are filtered to the current tuning only.
- Scale choices are filtered to the current tuning, except all-tuning choices
  such as `None`.
- The Tuning, Layout, and Scale pages mark the current choice with `*`.

Synth And MIDI

- Shared setting: `Synth Options` are board-wide and are also used by
  Sequencer mode where applicable.
- `Synth Mode` choices are `Off`, `Mono`, `Arp'gio`, and `Poly`.
- `Waveform` choices are `Hybrid`, `Square`, `Saw`, `Triangl`, `Sine`,
  `Strings`, and `Clrinet`.
- Arpeggiator timing uses `Arp BPM` and `Arp Speed`.
- Shared setting: `MIDI Options` are board-wide and are also used by
  Sequencer mode where applicable.
- `MPE Mode` choices are `Auto`, `Disable`, and `Force`.
- `MPE Bend` choices are `1`, `2`, `12`, `24`, `48`, and `96` semitones.
- The MIDI page also includes `MPE Low Ch`, `MPE High Ch`, `MPE Low Priority`,
  `Extra MPE`, `CC 74 Value`, `RolandMT32`, and `GeneralMidi`.

Profiles And Persistence

- There are 9 profile slots total:
  `Boot/Auto-Save Slot` and `Slot 1` through `Slot 8`.
- `Auto-Save` waits about 10 seconds after a settings change, then writes the
  current settings into the `Boot/Auto-Save Slot`.
- Loading a profile makes it the active runtime profile.
- `Reset Defaults` restores all profile slots to factory defaults.
- Settings are stored in onboard flash in `/settings.dat`.

Display And Advanced

- Shared setting: `DisplayNotes` shows a `Now Playing` overlay when notes are
  pressed.
- The overlay can wake the OLED temporarily if it was sleeping.
- The screen saver dims the OLED after about 33 seconds of inactivity.
- `Delegate Control` turns the board into an externally driven button/LED
  surface.
- `Update Firmware` reboots into the bootloader.

Entering And Leaving Sequencer

- Choose `Sequencer` from the Keyboard main menu to enter Sequencer mode.
- In Sequencer mode, choose `Keyboard` from the Sequencer menu to return.
