HEXBOARD PLAYING AND EXPRESSION REQUIREMENTS

Confidence

Overall confidence for this file is medium because the behavior is inferred
from current note-input, wheel, synth, and panic-stop code paths.

ID: KB-PLAY-010
Title: Playable Key Note Output
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: High

Requirement:
In Keyboard mode, pressing an allowed playable key shall trigger note output.

Details:
- MIDI note output shall be sent when the key resolves to a valid note.
- Onboard synth output shall also be triggered when `Synth Mode` is not `Off`.
- Releasing the key shall send the corresponding note-off behavior.

ID: KB-PLAY-011
Title: Scale Lock Play Gating
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: High

Requirement:
`Scale Lock` shall determine whether out-of-scale playable keys are allowed to
sound.

Details:
- When `Scale Lock` is On, only in-scale keys shall respond.
- When `Scale Lock` is Off, all playable note keys shall respond.

Related:
- KB-CORE-006
- KB-TUNE-024


ID: KB-PLAY-012
Title: Velocity Wheel Command Buttons
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: High

Requirement:
Keyboard mode shall provide a dedicated command-button velocity wheel.

Details:
- The top 3 command buttons shall make up the velocity wheel.
- The current velocity wheel value shall be used as played note velocity.
- The same current value shall also be used where the firmware emits channel
  pressure for MPE-style note handling.

Related:
- KB-UI-044
- KB-MIDI-053


ID: KB-PLAY-013
Title: Lower Wheel Toggle And Output
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: High

Requirement:
Keyboard mode shall provide a lower command-button wheel that can switch
between modulation and pitch bend.

Details:
- The center command button shall toggle the lower wheel mode.
- The lower 3 command buttons shall control the currently selected lower wheel.
- Modulation messages shall target the primary MIDI channel.
- Pitch bend changes shall target the primary MIDI channel and update synth
  frequency as needed.

Related:
- KB-PLAY-015
- KB-MIDI-050


ID: KB-PLAY-014
Title: Synth Mode Choices
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Keyboard `Synth Options` page shall provide selectable onboard synth modes.

Details:
- Current choices are `Off`, `Mono`, `Arp'gio`, and `Poly`.
- `Off` disables onboard synth note playback.
- `Mono` keeps one synth voice active at a time.
- `Arp'gio` cycles held notes according to arpeggiator timing.
- `Poly` uses multiple synth voices at once.

Notes:
- Shared setting: this page is board-wide and also affects Sequencer mode where
  applicable.

Related:
- KB-MENU-031
- KB-LIMIT-060


ID: KB-PLAY-015
Title: Wheel Speed And Behavior Settings
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Keyboard mode shall provide stored response settings for velocity, modulation,
and pitch-bend wheel movement.

Details:
- `Vel Wheel`, `PB Wheel`, and `Mod Wheel` shall each have a stored speed
  setting.
- Pitch bend and modulation shall each have a stored `Springy` or `Sticky`
  behavior setting.

Related:
- KB-MENU-033


ID: KB-PLAY-016
Title: Arpeggiator Timing From BPM And Division
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
In `Arp'gio` synth mode, the arpeggiator step duration shall be calculated from
the stored `Arp BPM` and `Arp Speed` settings.

Details:
- `Arp BPM` shall define the base tempo.
- `Arp Speed` shall define the whole-note division.
- A zero BPM or zero division shall be guarded against by falling back to `1`.

Related:
- KB-MENU-031
- KB-LIMIT-064


ID: KB-PLAY-017
Title: Panic Stop From Encoder Hold
Status: Active
Area: Playing And Expression
Priority: High

Requirement:
Holding the encoder for about 2 seconds shall perform a panic stop.

Details:
- The panic stop shall silence MIDI and synth output.
- It shall clear held note state and LED activity associated with current note
  playback.

Related:
- KB-UI-045
