HEXBOARD SCREENS AND FEEDBACK REQUIREMENTS

Confidence

Overall confidence for this file is medium-high because these items are derived
from the menu-label refresh, display, overlay, LED, and screen-saver code.

ID: KB-UI-040
Title: Current Choice Marker On Tuning, Layout, And Scale Pages
Status: Active
Area: Screens And Feedback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Keyboard Tuning, Layout, and Scale pages shall visibly mark the current
choice.

Details:
- The current choice row shall be prefixed with `* `.
- Opening each of those pages shall place the cursor on the current visible
  choice.

- KB-TUNE-022
- KB-TUNE-024


ID: KB-UI-041
Title: Color And Brightness Feedback Controls
Status: Active
Area: Screens And Feedback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
The Keyboard `Color Options` page shall expose stored LED color and brightness
controls.

Details:
- Current items are `Color Mode`, `Brightness`, `Animation`, `Rest Bright`, and
  `Dim Bright`.
- Previewing those values shall immediately refresh LED presentation where the
  current code supports preview callbacks.

Related:
- KB-MENU-030


ID: KB-UI-042
Title: DisplayNotes Overlay
Status: Active
Area: Screens And Feedback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
When the shared `DisplayNotes` setting is enabled, Keyboard mode shall provide
a temporary `Now Playing` overlay for played notes.

Details:
- The overlay shall show up to 6 unique notes.
- It shall stay visible briefly after release.
- It shall use a short chord-shrink grace period to avoid jitter when notes are
  released at slightly different times.

Notes:
- Shared setting: this overlay setting also affects note-display behavior in
  Sequencer mode where applicable.

Related:
- KB-LIMIT-061


ID: KB-UI-043
Title: Screen Saver Timeout
Status: Active
Area: Screens And Feedback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
The OLED display shall enter a low-contrast screen-saver state after a period
of inactivity.

Details:
- The current timeout is about 33 seconds.
- Keyboard interaction and menu activity shall reset the timer.
- The note overlay may temporarily wake the display.

Related:
- KB-UI-042


ID: KB-UI-044
Title: Encoder And Menu Shortcut Controls
Status: Active
Area: Screens And Feedback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Keyboard mode shall provide encoder-based menu navigation plus a temporary
command-button menu shortcut.

Details:
- Turning the encoder shall move through menu items or edit the current value.
- Clicking the encoder shall confirm or open the current selection.
- Holding the lowest command button below the encoder while a menu page is open
  shall make the top two command buttons act as menu up/down shortcuts.

Related:
- KB-CORE-003
- KB-PLAY-012


ID: KB-UI-045
Title: Encoder Hold Panic Gesture
Status: Active
Area: Screens And Feedback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Holding the encoder for about 2 seconds shall trigger the panic-stop gesture.

Details:
- The panic gesture shall not also trigger a normal encoder click afterward.

Related:
- KB-PLAY-017
