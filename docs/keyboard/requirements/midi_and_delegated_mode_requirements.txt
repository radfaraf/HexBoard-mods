ID: KB-MIDI-050
Title: Primary MIDI Routing
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: High

Requirement:
Keyboard mode shall provide MIDI note output using either primary-channel or
MPE-based routing, depending on current mode needs.

Details:
- When MPE is not active, the board shall use the configured default MIDI
  channel.
- When MPE is active, channel 1 shall act as the master channel.

ID: KB-MIDI-051
Title: MPE Mode Auto, Disable, And Force
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: High

Requirement:
The `MPE Mode` setting shall provide `Auto`, `Disable`, and `Force` behavior.

Details:
- `Auto` shall use single-channel routing when the tuning behaves like standard
  semitone steps and MPE is not otherwise required.
- `Auto` shall enable MPE when tuning or just-intonation behavior requires
  independent pitch control.
- `Disable` shall force non-MPE routing and enable the standard multi-channel
  microtonal fallback when needed.
- `Force` shall always enable MPE.

ID: KB-MIDI-052
Title: MPE Range And Channel Allocation
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: High

Requirement:
When MPE is enabled, the firmware shall use the configured MPE bend range and
playable channel window.

Details:
- `MPE Bend` shall define the pitch-bend range for playable MPE channels.
- `MPE Low Ch` and `MPE High Ch` shall define the playable MPE channel window.
- `MPE Low Priority` may force queue-based channel reuse behavior.
- The firmware shall advertise the current MPE zone and reset per-channel pitch
  bend ranges after a routing reset.

Related:
- KB-LIMIT-065


ID: KB-MIDI-053
Title: Extra MPE Messages
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
When `Extra MPE` is enabled, per-note output shall also send the extra
controller messages configured by the firmware.

Details:
- Current extra messages are channel pressure and `CC 74`.
- The `CC 74 Value` menu item shall provide the stored CC 74 payload.

Related:
- KB-PLAY-012


ID: KB-MIDI-054
Title: Program Change Preset Menus
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
The Keyboard MIDI page shall provide preset program-change selectors for
`RolandMT32` and `GeneralMidi`.

Details:
- Selecting a non-`None` entry shall store the chosen program number.
- The firmware shall send the program change on the primary MIDI channel after
  the setting changes.

ID: KB-MIDI-055
Title: External MIDI Note Tracking For Board Feedback
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: Low

Requirement:
Incoming external MIDI note-on and note-off events shall update the board's
external-note tracking state for affected hexes.

Details:
- This tracked state feeds current board feedback behavior such as the `MIDI In`
  animation mode.

ID: KB-MIDI-056
Title: Delegated Control Mode
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
The `Advanced` page shall provide a delegated-control mode that turns the board
into an externally driven button-and-LED surface.

Details:
- In delegated mode, most normal Keyboard behavior shall be disabled.
- Command buttons shall lose their normal wheel meanings.
- The menu may remain visible even though most normal menu actions are not
  useful until delegated mode is exited.

Related:
- KB-MIDI-057


ID: KB-MIDI-057
Title: Delegated SysEx And Button Event Protocol
Status: Active
Area: MIDI And Delegated Mode
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Delegated mode shall expose the current MIDI and SysEx control protocol used by
the firmware.

Details:
- Buttons shall be numbered `0` through `139`.
- Button press shall send `NoteOn` and release shall send `NoteOff`.
- The channel shall encode the hundreds digit and the MIDI note shall encode
  the remainder.
- The firmware shall accept delegated enter, exit, and LED-control SysEx codes.
- The firmware shall also answer the standard identity-request SysEx path it
  currently supports.
