HEXBOARD SEQUENCER PLAYBACK AND TIMING REQUIREMENTS

ID: SEQ-PLAY-001
Title: Step Clocking
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The sequencer shall treat each step as a 16th-note step at the current tempo.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-002
Title: Tempo Control
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a tempo control.

Details:
- Tempo is stored in the sequence file.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-003
Title: Active Playback Length
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a Steps control that sets the active playback
range from 1 to 32 steps.

Details:
- Step LEDs for steps beyond the active playback range shall remain off even if
  those steps still contain notes.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-004
Title: Direction Modes
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide the direction modes Forward, Backward,
Ping-Pong, Random, Brownian, and Drunk.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-005
Title: Tap Preview Control
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a Tap Preview setting with On and Off states.

Details:
- Off means tapping a step only selects it.
- On means tapping a step selects it and previews its stored notes.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-006
Title: Play Type Control
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a Play Type setting with MIDI and OB Synth
choices.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-007
Title: Length Value Set
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Quick length editing shall use the current supported length values.

Details:
- Supported values are:
  0, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800,
  900, 1000 percent.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-008
Title: Exact Length Entry Range
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Exact length entry shall allow direct numeric entry up to 4 digits and shall
clamp the saved value to 1000 percent maximum.

Details:
- The first digit typed replaces the current shown value.
- Backspace removes the previous digit.
- Cancel exits without saving changes.
- Encoder press saves and returns to the tools picker.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-009
Title: Velocity Range
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Step velocity shall support the range 0 through 127.

Details:
- Default step velocity is 96.
- Velocity is stored per step.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-010
Title: Velocity Editing Values
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Exact velocity editing shall use coarse encoder values of 0, 5, 10, 15 and so
on up to 125, plus 127.

Details:
- Encoder press saves and returns to the tools picker.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-011
Title: Note Order
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Step notes shall be stored and displayed in ascending pitch order.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-012
Title: Chord Playback
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Playback of a step shall send all notes stored in that step together.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-013
Title: Step Velocity Use
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The stored velocity value of a step shall affect sequencer playback, tap
preview, and live note audition while a step is selected.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-014
Title: Zero Length Behavior
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
A step with a length value of 0 percent shall not sound during playback.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-015
Title: Overlength Step Behavior
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
A step with a length value above 100 percent shall be allowed to continue
across later steps.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-016
Title: Probability Range
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Step probability shall support the range 0 through 100 percent.

Details:
- Default step probability is 100 percent.
- Probability is stored per step.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-017
Title: Probability Editing Values
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Exact probability editing shall use coarse encoder values of 0, 5, 10, 15 and
so on up to 100 percent.

Details:
- Encoder press saves and returns to the tools picker.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-018
Title: Step Probability Use
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The stored probability value of a step shall control the playback chance of
that step during sequencer transport playback.

Details:
- Probability applies to the whole step rather than individual notes.
- 0 percent means the step never sounds during transport playback.
- 100 percent means the step always sounds during transport playback.
- Probability applies to both MIDI and OB Synth playback modes.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-019
Title: Tap Preview Probability Bypass
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Tap Preview and manual step preview shall ignore step probability and always
audition the step's stored notes when preview is triggered.
