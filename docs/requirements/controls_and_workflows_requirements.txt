HEXBOARD SEQUENCER CONTROLS AND WORKFLOWS REQUIREMENTS

ID: SEQ-CTRL-001
Title: Step Pad Mapping
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The first 8 playable keys of the first 4 rows shall act as sequencer step pads.

Details:
- Row 1 maps to steps 1 through 8.
- Row 2 maps to steps 9 through 16.
- Row 3 maps to steps 17 through 24.
- Row 4 maps to steps 25 through 32.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-002
Title: Play Stop Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The first-row last button shall act as the Play/Stop key.

Details:
- A short press toggles transport.
- A hold of about 2 seconds opens the performance monitor while held.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-003
Title: Overview Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The second-row second-to-last button shall page the step overview overlay.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-004
Title: Blue Action Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The second-row last button shall act as the blue action key.

Details:
- A short press shall undo the currently selected step back to the state it had
  when selected.
- A hold of about 1 second shall clear the selected step.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-005
Title: Step Tools Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The third-row last button shall open the step tools picker for the currently
selected step.

Details:
- If no step is selected, the system shall show a status message instead of
  opening the picker.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-006
Title: Step Selection
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Pressing a step pad shall select that step.

Details:
- Pressing the same selected step again shall deselect it.
- The selected step shall visually blink to show selection.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-007
Title: Lower Keyboard Audition
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The lower keyboard area shall remain playable in Sequencer mode for note
audition.

Details:
- With no step selected, pressing a lower keyboard note shall audition only.
- With a step selected, pressing a lower keyboard note shall audition and also
  toggle that note in the selected step.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-008
Title: Quick Length Edit
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Turning the encoder while a step is selected shall change that step's length
through the predefined length choices.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-009
Title: Encoder Return To Menu
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Pressing the encoder while a step is selected shall deselect the step and
return to the normal Sequencer menu view.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-010
Title: Naming Save Control
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Pressing the encoder during naming mode shall save the current file or folder
name entry.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-011
Title: Step Tools Palette
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The step tools picker shall provide the tools Len, Vel, Oct+, Oct-, Prob, Tie,
and Cancel/Finished.

Details:
- Len opens exact length entry.
- Vel opens exact velocity entry.
- Oct+ transposes the selected step up one octave.
- Oct- transposes the selected step down one octave.
- Prob is a placeholder for future probability editing.
- Tie is a placeholder for future tie behavior.
- Cancel/Finished exits the tools picker.
